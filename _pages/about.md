I am a Ph.D. candidate in computational biology at the [National Genomics Data Center (NGDC)](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/), [China National Center for Bioinformation (CNCB)](https://www.cncb.ac.cn/), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **bioinformatics**, **deep learning**, **single-cell transcriptomics**, and **computational immunology**. I develop and apply data-driven computational methods to understand complex biological systems, with a particular focus on decoding the immune microenvironment from high-throughput single-cell data.

{: .notice--info}
我是国家基因组科学数据中心（国家生物信息中心，中国科学院）的计算生物学博士研究生。研究方向为**生物信息学、深度学习、单细胞转录组与计算免疫学**，致力于开发数据驱动的计算方法，从高通量单细胞数据中解析免疫微环境。
